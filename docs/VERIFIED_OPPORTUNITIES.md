## Notes

- **PM Youth loan scheme:** loan amounts and markup rates were deliberately left out. The State Bank of
  Pakistan revised the scheme on 26 Nov 2026 (new higher tier), so users are sent to the official portal
  for current limits.
- **DigiSkills Batch-05:** has no official last date (enrollment closes when the 300,000 seats fill).
  Set its status to *closed* in the admin dashboard once the batch starts on 1 Dec 2026.
- **Kissan Card:** registration is by SMS to 8070, so there is no application website.
- The 10 sample opportunities marked **[DEMO]** were **unpublished** (hidden from the app, not deleted)
  on 27 Nov 2026, so the app now shows only real, verified listings.

## How a listing gets verified

1. A staff member adds the listing in the admin dashboard with the official source and application links.
2. It starts as *pending verification* and is not visible in the app.
3. A reviewer opens the official pages and confirms the source, application link, deadline, eligibility
   and fees in the verification checklist, adds a note of what was checked, then approves and publishes it.
